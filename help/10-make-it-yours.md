# Making it yours

All of this is in [Preferences](#do=preferences).

- **Language:** English, Spanish, French or German, under General.
- **Themes and fonts:** under Appearance, with **Customize Theme…** for your own accent, panel and background colours.
  Text on your panels changes to stay readable. Panel and background colours stay in the app, so exports don't use them,
  and High Contrast keeps its own. With Glass on, panels stay solid over colours you've chosen.
- **Add-ons:** under Appearance. They add code-block types, export formats and themes.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** under Shortcuts, where you can change any of them.
- **Menu bar:** under General, choose whether it always shows.

This Help Center is in English for now.
