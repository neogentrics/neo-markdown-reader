# Reading comfortably

## Themes and text

Click the moon button in the toolbar to change the theme, look, typeface and page width. There are ten themes, and **Glass** turns any of them to frosted glass. Choose from fifteen fonts, including Times New Roman, Georgia and Century.

For colour blindness, turn on **Colour-safe colours** in [Preferences → Appearance](#do=preferences:appearance). Compare, charts and highlights change to colours that stay different, in any theme. Make the text larger or smaller with the **A** buttons, or `Ctrl+=` and `Ctrl+-`. The same choices are in [Preferences → Appearance](#do=preferences:appearance).

## Moving around

- **Outline:** `Ctrl+B` shows the document's headings. Click one to jump to it.
- **Go to a file or heading:** `Ctrl+K`.
- **Find:** `Ctrl+F`.
- **Tabs:** every file opens in a tab. Right-click a tab for more choices.
- **Split view:** `Ctrl+\` shows two documents side by side.

## Highlights and notes

Select some text and press `Ctrl+Shift+H`. Highlights are private: they're kept by the app and never written into the file. See them all in the **Notes** panel.

## Staying focused

**View → Focus Mode** (`F8`) hides everything but the page. **View → Present as Slides** turns a document into a presentation.

The app reopens your tabs where you stopped reading. You can turn that off in [Preferences → General](#do=preferences:general).
