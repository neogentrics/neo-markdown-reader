# Reading comfortably

## Themes and text

Click the moon button in the toolbar to change the theme, look, typeface and page width. There are ten themes, and **Glass** turns any of them to frosted glass. Choose from fifteen fonts, including Times New Roman, Georgia and Century.

For colour blindness, turn on **Colour-safe colours** in [Preferences → Appearance](#do=preferences:appearance). Compare, charts and highlights change to colours that stay different, in any theme. Make the text larger or smaller with the **A** buttons, or `Ctrl+=` and `Ctrl+-`. The same choices are in [Preferences → Appearance](#do=preferences:appearance).

## Moving around

- **Outline:** `Ctrl+B` shows the document's headings. Click one to jump to it.
- **Go to a file or heading:** `Ctrl+K`.
- **Find:** `Ctrl+F`.
- **Tabs:** every file opens in a tab. Right-click a tab for more choices.
- **Split view:** `Ctrl+\` shows two documents side by side.

## Highlights and notes

Select some text and press `Ctrl+Shift+H`. Highlights are private: they're kept by the app and never written into the file. See them all in the **Notes** panel.

## Flashcards

Write cards in your document, and choose *View → Study Flashcards* to learn them.

```
Q: What does DNS do?
A: It turns names into addresses.

Firewall
: Filters traffic between networks.
```

Bold terms in a list count too, under a heading such as *Glossary*, *Key Terms* or *Vocabulary*.

- **Start Studying** shows each card that's due. Choose how well you knew it (or press `1` to `4`), and cards you know well come back less often. **Again** brings a card back in a few cards' time.
- **Practice All** goes through every card, and **Quiz Me** asks multiple-choice questions using the other answers. Neither changes when cards come back.
- With a folder open, you can study every card in it.

The words of your cards stay in your document. The app keeps only the date each one comes back, so changing an answer keeps its place, and changing a question starts that card again. Progress isn't kept for locked documents or ones you haven't saved yet.

## Staying focused

**View → Focus Mode** (`F8`) hides everything but the page. **View → Present as Slides** turns a document into a presentation. A section too long for one slide flows onto as many as it needs. Move with the arrow keys, by clicking, or with the mouse wheel.

The app reopens your tabs where you stopped reading. You can turn that off in [Preferences → General](#do=preferences:general).
