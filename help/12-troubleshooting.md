# Troubleshooting

## Windows says it protected your PC

The installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## I can't find a menu item

Press `Ctrl+Shift+K` and type its name. Settings moved to [Preferences](#do=preferences) in 3.4.1, and their shortcuts still work.

## The voice sounds robotic

That's your computer's own voice. See [Voices and reading aloud](#help=voices).

## Claude can't find my documents

Check the app is open, and that the connector is on in [Connect Claude](#do=connect-claude). After adding it, restart Claude Desktop or start a new Claude Code session.

## Claude Code says it isn't signed in

Run `claude` in a terminal, sign in, and try again.

## A pop-up says "Font Capture"

That message comes from Adobe Acrobat or Reader, not this app. Update Acrobat and Windows, then restart.

## Something else

Copy your version from [About](#do=about) and [report it](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-markdown-reader/issues/new).
