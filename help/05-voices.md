# Voices and reading aloud

**View → Read Aloud** (`Ctrl+Shift+U`) reads the document to you and marks each sentence as it goes. A bar in the window has play, skip by section or paragraph, speed and voice. Drag it anywhere, by its handle or any empty part of it. Double-click it to put it back at the bottom.

[Start reading aloud](#do=read-aloud)

## Changing the voice

Open [Preferences → Reading Aloud](#do=preferences:voices), or use the **Voice** list in the bar while reading. You can choose:

- **This computer's voices**, which are already installed. They can sound robotic.
- **Natural voices**: ten voices that sound like a person. Clara, Maya, Nora, Sofia and Zoe are female, and Ethan, Leo, Marcus, Owen and Theo are male.

## Getting the natural voices

1. Open the **Voice** list.
2. Choose **Get natural voices…** at the top.
3. Agree to the download, about 400 MB, one time only.
4. Pick a voice.

Every sentence is made on your computer, so nothing you read is sent anywhere, and they work without internet. They read 31 languages. To remove them, choose **Remove natural voices…** at the end of the same list.

## Choosing your speakers

Open [Preferences → Reading Aloud](#do=preferences:voices) and pick a device under **Play through**. This works with the natural voices. The computer plays its own voices, so they always use its default device.

## Reading through slides

In a presentation, press **R**. Reading marks each sentence and turns the slides by itself. Press the arrow keys to move to another slide and the reading comes with you. Press **Esc** to leave the slides, and it carries on in the document.

## Teaching it how to say a word

[Open the pronunciation list](#do=pronunciation), type the word, then spell it the way it sounds. Press **Listen** to try it. Choose **All documents**, or just the documents in this folder and the folders inside it. It's used for reading aloud and for audio exports.
