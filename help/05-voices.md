# Voices and reading aloud

**View → Read Aloud** (`Ctrl+Shift+U`) reads the document to you and marks each sentence as it goes. A bar at the bottom of the window has play, skip, speed and voice.

[Start reading aloud](#do=read-aloud)

## Changing the voice

Open [Preferences → Reading Aloud](#do=preferences:voices), or use the **Voice** list in the bar while reading. You can choose:

- **This computer's voices**, which are already installed. They can sound robotic.
- **Natural voices**: ten voices, five female and five male, that sound like a person.

## Getting the natural voices

1. Open the **Voice** list.
2. Choose **Get natural voices…** at the top.
3. Agree to the download, about 400 MB, one time only.
4. Pick a voice.

Every sentence is made on your computer, so nothing you read is sent anywhere, and they work without internet. They read 31 languages. To remove them, choose **Remove natural voices…** at the end of the same list.
