# Exporting and printing

Click the export button in the toolbar, or open **File → Export**:

| Format | Good for |
|---|---|
| PDF | Sharing and printing, with choices for paper, cover and contents |
| Word (.docx) | Editing in Word |
| Web page (.html) | One self-contained file |
| Plain text | Pasting anywhere |
| E-book (.epub) | E-readers |
| Slides | PDF or PowerPoint, split at each `---` |

**Print** is `Ctrl+P`.

## Your own look

[Customize the theme](#do=customize-theme) to set your colours and fonts. Exports use them too.

**Copy as Formatted Text** (`Ctrl+Shift+C`) pastes into Word or an email with its formatting.

## Audio

**File → Export → Audio (MP3 or Audiobook)…** has the natural voices read the whole document into a file. An MP3 is one file, and an audiobook (`.m4b`) has a chapter for each main heading.

[Export as audio](#do=export-audio)

It uses your reading speed and pronunciation list. It runs in the background, with its progress in the corner of the window, and you can keep working. A long book can take hours. Audiobooks can't be made on Linux, so it offers MP3 there.
