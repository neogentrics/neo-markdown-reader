# Common questions

<details><summary>How do I change the voice?</summary>

Open [Preferences → Reading Aloud](#do=preferences:voices). Natural voices are a free download from the Voice list. [More about voices](#help=voices)
</details>

<details><summary>Where did the settings in the menus go?</summary>

They're all in [Preferences](#do=preferences), `Ctrl+,`. Their shortcuts still work, and `Ctrl+Shift+K` finds anything by name.
</details>

<details><summary>Why can't I type in my document?</summary>

Documents open read-only. Click **Edit** first. [More about editing](#help=editing)
</details>

<details><summary>How do I add my AI key?</summary>

[Open AI Assist Settings](#do=ai-settings), pick a service and paste your key. [Step by step](#help=ai)
</details>

<details><summary>Do I need an AI service to use the app?</summary>

No. AI assist is optional, and off until you turn it on.
</details>

<details><summary>Is my writing sent anywhere?</summary>

Not unless you ask for something that sends it. [Privacy and safety](#help=privacy)
</details>

<details><summary>How do I get back an earlier version?</summary>

Open **Edit → Compare and History → Version History**, or press `Ctrl+Shift+Y`.
</details>

<details><summary>How do I update the app?</summary>

It updates itself. When a new version is ready, choose **Help → Restart to Update**.
</details>
