# Getting started

RT Markdown Reader opens `.md` files as clean, well-laid-out pages. Double-click a Markdown file, drag one onto the window, or press `Ctrl+O` (`Cmd+O` on a Mac).

**The fastest way to learn the app is the [tutorial](#do=tutorial).** It takes a few minutes and uses a practice document.

## Finding your way around

- **The toolbar** has the things you use most: Open, Edit, find, text size, theme, export and print.
- **The menus** hold commands. Settings live in [Preferences](#do=preferences), which is `Ctrl+,` or the gear button.
- **The sidebar** (`Ctrl+B`) has the Outline, Files, Search, Tasks, Notes and History panels.
- **Find a Command** (`Ctrl+Shift+K`) finds anything in the app by name, including items that aren't in the menus.
