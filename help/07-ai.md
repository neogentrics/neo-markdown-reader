# Connecting an AI service

AI assist can summarize a document, explain a passage, proofread, rewrite, translate, and answer questions about a folder. It's optional. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose a service, turn it on and ask for something.

![The AI Assist Settings window, with a list of services, a place for a key, and a list of models.](guide/ai-services.png)

[Open AI Assist Settings](#do=ai-settings), turn on AI assist, then pick one of these.

## With Claude Code

If Claude Code is installed and signed in, choose **Claude Code**. It uses your Claude plan, and no key is needed.

## With an API key

Choose Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral or OpenRouter, click **Get an API key**, and paste the key. Each service bills you directly. Your key is stored encrypted on this computer.

## With a model on your own computer

Install Ollama or LM Studio and download a model. Choose **On this computer** and click **Refresh list**. Nothing leaves your computer, and it works without internet.

## Using it

Find everything under **Edit → AI Assist**. Writing help appears as a marked-up change to accept or reject. Password-locked documents are never sent.
