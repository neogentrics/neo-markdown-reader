# Privacy and safety

- **Your documents stay on your computer** unless you ask for something that sends them, and the app says so first.
- **Files open read-only.** Editing asks first, and the original is backed up.
- **Highlights and notes** are private, and never written into the file.
- **Flashcard progress** holds dates and a fingerprint of each question, never the words of your cards.
- **Search by meaning** runs on your computer. Its index holds numbers, not your words, and locked documents are never read.
- **API keys** are stored encrypted by your operating system.
- **Code blocks** run only when you click Run, after the app asks, in a sealed sandbox. Turn it off in [Preferences → Editing](#do=preferences:editing).
- **Notion** gets your document only when you press Send, after seeing what will change. Linked documents ask Notion whether their page has changed, without sending any of your words. Your key goes to Notion and nowhere else.
- **Password-locked documents** are never sent to AI, shared with Claude, sent to Notion or synced.
