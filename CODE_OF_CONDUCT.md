# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here. Recon Towers projects are places to report problems and share ideas, so this page is short.

**Do**

- Be respectful and assume people mean well.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help others when you can.

**Don't**

- Harass, insult or threaten anyone.
- Post anything hateful, or anything sexual.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.

**If something goes wrong,** email author@neogentrics.com. Reports are kept private. Comments that break these rules
can be removed, and people who keep breaking them can be blocked.
