              Sync between computers
              
    
              Keep your highlights, notes, bookmarks, recent files, reading positions and settings the same on every computer, through a folder you already sync with OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. No account is needed: your cloud service moves the files.
              
    
              
    
              
      Sync folder
      
      
    
              
    
              
    
    
    
              
    
              Never synced: your AI key, editing permissions, version history, and anything about password-locked documents. Your documents themselves aren’t copied: keep them in a synced folder too, and their highlights follow them.
